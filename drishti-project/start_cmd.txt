METHOD 1: USING UI APPS (RECOMMENDED)
--------------------------------------
1. Open "XAMPP Control Panel" from your Windows Start menu or:
   C:\xampp\xampp-control.exe

2. In the XAMPP window, look for "MySQL" and click the "Start" button.
   (The MySQL label will turn GREEN and show Port 3306).

3. To view your database visually in your browser:
   Click the "Admin" button next to MySQL in XAMPP.
   This opens phpMyAdmin where you can visually inspect drishti_db.

4. To start the Drishti application:
   Simply double-click "Run_Drishti.bat" in this folder.
   It checks MySQL, launches the app, and opens your browser automatically!
