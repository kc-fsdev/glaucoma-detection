    PRIMARY OCULAR STRUCTURE

    Optic Nerve

    The visual cable transmitting ocular light impulses to the brain

    Biological Function

    The optic nerve is composed of over 1.2 million retinal ganglion cell axons. It carries electrical impulses generated by the photoreceptors of the retina directly to the visual cortex of the brain for image interpretation.

    Clinical Connection to Glaucoma Screening

    Glaucoma is primarily an optic neuropathy. When fluid pressure inside the eye (intraocular pressure) rises or when vascular perfusion is insufficient, the delicate nerve fibers within the optic nerve suffer irreversible progressive damage. In fundus image screening, eye care algorithms assess the health, color, and margin of the optic nerve head to detect early thinning or nerve fiber loss before peripheral vision is permanently degraded.
